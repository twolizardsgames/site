Short description

My Dream Dollhouse Shop is a cozy shop game: unpack tiny toys, fill the shelves, ring up happy customers and grow your shop room by room into a dollhouse of its own.

About the game

Unpack the delivery boxes, fill the shelves with tiny teacups, teddy bears and canopy beds, and ring up the happy customers who come to visit.

Every item you find goes into your collection and into the Dream Dollhouse in your shop window. The prettier it gets, the more people stop to peek in. Build new rooms, a stairwell and floor after floor, decorate every room, hire friendly helpers, and dress your shopkeeper your way. Old friends drop by with a gentle story that ends at the county fair.

Nothing ever goes wrong: no failing, no timers and no lost progress. There are no ads, no accounts and no chat, and the game collects no data. Every purchase asks for a grown-up first.

Features

  • Stock shelves, ring up customers and grant their wishes
  • 32 items in eight themes, each in three colors: 96 finds for the album
  • Grow from one room to a many-floored house with a spiral stairwell
  • Wallpapers, floors, rugs, curtains and corner pieces for every room
  • A shopkeeper creator with hairstyles, outfits and accessories
  • A twelve-visit story with friends from the neighborhood
  • Share a picture of your shop and a link friends can explore in 3D
  • At launch: a Christmas pack with a snowy street, twinkly lights, a snowman and a Christmas Tree Room

About the studio

Two Lizards Games is a one-person studio in California. My Dream Dollhouse Shop is its first game, play-tested with an 11-year-old whose feedback shaped how each new thing is taught: one tap at a time, right when it's needed.
