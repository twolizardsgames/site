My Dream Dollhouse Shop

Run a cozy little toy shop, collect tiny treasures and build the dollhouse of your dreams.

Order boxes of toys, carry them to the shelves, and help each customer find just the thing they wished for. Every new find goes in your collection, and your favorites go in your very own Dream Dollhouse in the shop window.

What you'll do

  • 🧸 Stock the shelvesOrder toys from the catalog, carry the boxes in and fill your shelves.
  • 💝 Make customers happyRing up sales, grant wishes and earn hearts and tips.

Made for kids, kind to grown-ups

  • Made for ages 11 to 15, and fun for anyone who likes cozy games.
  • No ads, no tracking, no accounts and no chat. The game collects no personal information (see the privacy policy).
  • Free to download, with optional one-time purchases: the Full shop and decoration packs. No subscriptions and no random paid rewards. Every purchase asks for a grown-up first.
  • Plays offline, in portrait, a few minutes at a time.
